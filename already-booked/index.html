Your ADHD assessment is booked. What do you need now?

Use the cards below to jump straight to what you need while you wait, prepare for the appointment and understand what happens afterwards.

If the assessment is for a child or teenager, use the dedicated children and young people guide.

Choose what you need

What do I do while I wait?

Waits, delays, checking what you actually booked, and cancelling or rearranging.

→

What will they actually ask me?

What an assessment involves, how long it may take, who takes part, and questionnaires or forms.

→

What should I have ready?

Examples, childhood information, medication, useful documents and anything you want to ask.

→

What do I do on the day?

How to describe your life accurately, what to say if a question doesn’t fit, and what to take with you.

→

It’s over. What now?

The outcome, including no diagnosis, plus reports, timings, errors and who is responsible for what next.

The assessment is for my child

The same stages, with school input, parent information and how a young person’s own view is included.
